Hey — I'm Alish, a first-year BIT student at Amrit Science Campus in Kathmandu. I'm starting this blog as a place to write down what I'm learning as I go: coursework notes, small projects, and things I get stuck on and eventually figure out.

## Why write this down

Most of what I build lives in scattered repos and half-finished notes. Writing it up properly forces me to actually understand it, and gives future-me (and maybe you) something to search for later.

## What to expect

A mix of things, roughly in this order of frequency:

- Notes from first-semester courses — C, discrete math, digital logic
- Small backend and full-stack experiments
- Write-ups on side projects as they progress

## How this blog works

No database, no CMS, no backend. Every post is a Markdown file in `_posts/`, and GitHub Pages' built-in Jekyll turns them into pages automatically. One shared layout controls how every post looks, so a single edit updates the whole blog at once.

```bash
_posts/2026-09-22-welcome-to-my-blog.md   # this post
```
