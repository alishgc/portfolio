I used to think you needed to be an expert before you could write or speak about a topic. I've since changed my mind.

## The old model

Learn privately → become an expert → teach.

## The new model

Learn publicly → teach what you just learned → learn from the teaching.

The second model has a few advantages:

- **It forces clarity.** You can't hand-wave when you're writing for an audience.
- **It creates feedback loops.** People correct you, ask questions, and point you to better resources.
- **It builds a record.** Future you will thank present you for writing things down.

## What "in public" means

It doesn't mean live-streaming every keystroke. It means:

1. Writing short notes as you go
2. Sharing drafts, not just finished pieces
3. Being honest about what you don't know

> The best time to write about something is when you're still learning it.
